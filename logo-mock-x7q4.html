What CSS can't do

The limits of retouching this raster in the browser — so the pick is made with open eyes.

The purple shift (C/D) is a rotation, not a repaint. hue-rotate spins the whole existing gradient at once — we can land the pink on violet and keep the orange as warm magenta, but we can't hold a true orange highlight AND a violet body in the same image. If the founder wants violet/indigo with genuinely orange-warm highlights, the source asset needs re-exporting with those colors.

The asset has no transparency (opaque black square), so glows are built from blurred duplicates + halos, which look right on dark night backgrounds — everywhere the app lives — but the same trick won't survive on a light surface (app-store listing, email header). A re-export with an alpha channel fixes both limits at once.

Recommendation: D — purple-shifted + glow. It moves the mark fully into the app's night palette while the bloom makes it read as the moon the product already leans on; B is the safe fallback if brand recognition of the pink-orange matters more right now.
