FAQ
What is DreamTube?
Type or record a description of a dream, and DreamTube turns it into a short AI-generated video — or, if you'd rather, a single AI-generated image instead.
How do tokens work?
A video generation — new, edited, or regenerated — costs 100 tokens; an image generation costs 10. You start with 320 free. Your first daily claim gives you 100 tokens, then 20 more each day after that (tap the token chip when it's glowing). Need more than that? You can buy token packs anytime in the Shop.
My generation failed. Do I get my tokens back?
Yes, automatically, every time. If something still seems off, contact support below and we'll sort it out.
How long does a video take to generate?
Usually under a minute or two — you'll see progress on screen while it renders.
Can I edit a dream after it's made?
Yes — change the text or style and regenerate. Each regenerate costs another 100 tokens for a video (or 10 for an image), same as making it the first time.
What's "Advanced"?
Optional extras before you generate: add characters (including yourself, via photo or description), pick a camera angle, and set the time/place of the scene.
Can I use my own photo?
Yes, for yourself specifically, when generating a video. Image generation is text-only — it doesn't use a photo, so describe yourself in words there instead. Photos of other people aren't supported yet, for safety reasons.
Is my dream public or private?
Private by default. You choose if and when to publish a dream to Explore, where other users can see it.
Can I delete a dream, or my account?
Yes to both. You can delete any dream you've created any time. You can also permanently delete your whole account — go to Settings > Danger zone > Delete account below, confirm with your password, and it's gone for good: your account, dreams, and token balance are all removed. (Billing history is kept separately by our payment processor, as usual.)
