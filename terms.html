What DreamTube is

DreamTube lets you turn a written or spoken description of a dream into a short AI-generated video or image, using fal.ai's models. You can keep results private or post them to the shared Explore feed.

Your account

You need an account (username, password, and ideally an email so we can reach you and let you reset your password) to generate dreams. You're responsible for keeping your password secure and for anything that happens under your account.

Tokens & purchases

Generating a video or image spends tokens from your balance. You get some tokens free on signup, plus a small number of free tokens you can claim once a day; you can buy more in the Shop. Purchases are processed by Dodo Payments; see our Privacy Policy for what that involves. Purchases are generally final — contact support if something went wrong with a charge or a generation failed and didn't refund automatically.

AI-generated content — no quality guarantee

Video and image generation uses third-party AI models (fal.ai). Results can vary in quality, may not always accurately reflect what you described, and may occasionally fail to generate at all. We don't guarantee any particular output, and generation failures are refunded automatically where possible (contact support if one isn't).

Acceptable use

Don't use DreamTube to generate or post content that is illegal, that depicts real minors in a sexual or exploitative way, that infringes someone else's rights, or that's intended to harass, defame, or impersonate someone without their consent. DreamTube doesn't support uploading a photo of anyone other than yourself for character generation, and photos of children/teens can't be used for generation at all. We can remove content or suspend accounts that violate this.

Reporting & blocking

If you see something in the Explore feed that shouldn't be there, tap the More action on that dream to report it — our team reviews every report. You can also block an account from that same menu; once blocked, that account's dreams stop showing up in your Explore feed. To reach us directly about a safety concern, use Settings → Support & feedback → Contact support — we read every message.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account, confirmed with your password. This is irreversible — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what is and isn't removed.
